# 🚀 MASTER PROMPT FOR Tioky.jp WEBSITE

Create a modern, mobile-first, high-conversion business website for a Japan-based company:

Company Name: Tioky LLC
Website: tioky.jp
Contact Email: [contact@tioky.jp](mailto:contact@tioky.jp)

---

🎯 CORE POSITIONING (VERY IMPORTANT)

This is NOT a general company website.

Position the business as:

“Japan Auction Export & Vehicle Buying Support Service”

Primary focus (80% importance):

* Japan used vehicle auction assistance
* Auction bidding support
* Export services worldwide

Secondary services (20% importance, supporting only):

* Website design
* SEO services
* Graphic design
* Digital marketing

⚠️ Do NOT present digital services as equal to export services.
They must appear as supporting capabilities that strengthen trust.

---

🎯 TARGET AUDIENCE

* International buyers (Africa, Asia, Australia, Canada)
* People searching:

  * “Japan car auction agent”
  * “buy cars from Japan auction”
  * “Japan export service”

---

🎯 WEBSITE GOAL

* Generate leads (Get Quote / Contact)
* Build trust with international buyers
* Clearly explain auction process
* Be simple, fast, and professional

---

📱 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

* Mobile-first responsive design
* Clean, minimal corporate style
* Colors: white, dark blue, light gray
* Typography: modern sans-serif (Noto Sans)
* Fast loading (no heavy animations or videos)
* Clear spacing, grid-based layout

---

🏠 PAGES TO GENERATE

1. HOME PAGE (conversion-focused)

Include:

* Hero: “Japan Auction Export & Vehicle Buying Support”
* Subtext: Helping international buyers purchase vehicles from Japan auctions and ship worldwide
* CTA buttons: “Get Quote” and “Contact Us”
* Trust section (why choose Tioky)
* Core services (auction + export)
* Simple 4-step process
* Contact section

Add a small section introducing:
“Digital & Business Support Services”
→ Keep it short and secondary

---

2. SERVICES PAGE

Split clearly into:

🚗 Japan Auction & Export Services (primary, detailed)

* Auction bidding support
* Export handling
* Cost estimation
* Consultation

💻 Digital Services (secondary, shorter)

* Website design
* SEO
* Graphic design
* Digital marketing

---

3. HOW IT WORKS PAGE

Simple 4 steps:

1. Find vehicle
2. Auction bidding
3. Payment & processing
4. Shipping worldwide

---

4. CONTACT PAGE

Simple form:

* Name
* Email
* Country
* Message

Include:

* [contact@tioky.jp](mailto:contact@tioky.jp)
* WhatsApp click-to-chat button (mobile sticky)

---

5. ABOUT PAGE

Explain:

* Japan-based company
* Focus on export services
* Also provides digital solutions

Tone:

* Honest, simple, trustworthy
* No exaggerated claims

---

6. BLOG PAGE (SEO READY)

Include blog structure for future posts targeting:

* Japan car auction keywords
* Export guides
* Cost breakdowns
* Auction tips

---

🔍 SEO REQUIREMENTS

Optimize for keywords:

* Japan car auction export
* used car Japan export service
* auction agent Japan
* buy cars from Japan auction

Add:

* Meta titles and descriptions for each page
* Clean URL structure
* Basic schema markup (Organization)

---

📈 CONVERSION FEATURES

* Sticky WhatsApp button (mobile)
* “Get Quote” CTA visible across pages
* Simple forms (no long forms)
* Clear sections, no clutter

---

🧠 TRUST STRATEGY

Include:

* Clear explanation of process
* International customer focus
* Simple, realistic language
* Avoid generic marketing phrases

Add subtle trust line:
“Our digital expertise helps us support global clients efficiently.”

---

⚡ PERFORMANCE

* Lightweight design
* Optimized images
* Fast loading (<2 seconds)

---

🚀 FUTURE-READY STRUCTURE

Ensure the website can expand later with:

* SEO blog content
* Online tools (cost calculator, auction tools)
* Landing pages for different countries

---

🧭 FINAL OUTPUT STYLE

* Clean, professional, minimal
* Conversion-focused (not decorative)
* Structured like a SaaS + export service hybrid
* Clear hierarchy: Export services first, digital services second

---

DO NOT:

* Overload homepage with text
* Mix services randomly
* Use flashy animations
* Make it look like a generic agency site

---

END GOAL:

A high-trust, lead-generating website that ranks for Japan auction export services and converts international buyers.
